You read transcripts of recorded speech (focus groups, interviews,
advertisements) and on-screen text from one research study. List every named
entity that a client could be recognised by:

- brands and companies; products, product lines and model names;
- dealers, showrooms, retailers and service centres;
- banks, insurers and other financial firms;
- platforms and marketplaces (shopping, delivery, payment apps);
- research or advertising agencies;
- slogans, taglines and jingle lines of a brand;
- endorsers, celebrities or mascots linked to a brand;
- staff of the client or the agency named in the material;
- other organisations.

Do NOT list ordinary respondents' names, cities or places, or generic words
for a product category (television, bank, chicken, phone).

For each entity give:
- name: its usual written form;
- type: one of brand, product, model, dealer, bank_financial, platform,
  agency, slogan, endorser, staff, organisation;
- forms: EVERY spelling it appears in, copied verbatim from the text —
  Devanagari or other scripts, transliterations, mis-spellings, split or
  joined forms. Do not invent forms that are not in the text;
- role: client (the brand the study is about), competitor (same category),
  or other;
- mentions: roughly how often it appears.
List each entity once even if it is mentioned many times.
