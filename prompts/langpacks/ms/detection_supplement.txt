LANGUAGE SUPPLEMENT — MALAY / INDONESIAN (Bahasa Melayu / Bahasa Indonesia),
usually CODE-SWITCHED with English mid-sentence. All categories and global
rules above apply unchanged; this calibrates observed under-detection in this
language pair. ALL of the following ARE findings (examples are synthetic):

- ORGANIZATION_PERSON_LINKED: a bank, lender, fund, agency or comparison
  service named as handling THIS speaker's own account, loan, card,
  application or case — "saya ada loan dengan Alliance Bank", "kad UOB
  saya", "saya apply melalui RinggitPlus", "duit KWSP saya", "saya pergi
  AKPK", "pinjaman PTPTN saya", "guna Maybank punya app". Rule 4 applies
  with force: once the person link is established in the call, EVERY later
  mention of that institution ("UOB punya offer", "yang Alliance tu",
  "dekat BSN") is a separate span. Only a bare institution name in general
  talk with no person link anywhere in the call is excluded.
- FINANCIAL_INFO: Malay word-form amounts are values — "seribu" (= satu
  ribu), "seribu lima" (1500), "empat ribu tiga ratus", "dua puluh ribu" —
  including hedged colloquial forms: "seribu lah kot", "seribu lebih",
  "tiga ribu macam tu", "dalam lima ribu". Hedge words (lah, kot, lebih,
  macam tu, dalam, kut) join the span when they bound or interrupt the
  amount. Salary (gaji), instalment (ansuran), debt (hutang), balance,
  limit and spending amounts are personal financial facts.
- CHILD_INFO: dependants statements — "empat orang anak", "anak saya dua
  orang", "anak tiga lah", "anak I pergi sekolah [named school]" — mark the
  count/school span; stacks with the specific category.
- FULL_NAME: Malay name particles and chains — Wan, Nik, Che, Syed,
  Tengku, bin/binti chains ("Syed Afiq bin Wan Ahmad"). A single
  particle-name used to address or identify a real person ("Encik Wan",
  "ya, Afiq") is a FULL_NAME finding (exclude the separable honorific).
- IMMIGRATION_STATUS: explicit residency/PR statements in Malay — "saya
  bukan permanent resident", "tak ada pegang mana-mana PR dekat negara
  luar", "saya warganegara Malaysia" (the latter is NATIONALITY).
- SENSITIVE_DEMOGRAPHIC: explicit person-linked ethnicity/religion words —
  "saya Chinese", "dia Melayu", "orang India" when stating a specific
  person's ethnicity (never inferred from name or language).
- Spoken digits in Malay/Indonesian count everywhere digits count: kosong/
  nol, satu, dua, tiga, empat, lima, enam, tujuh, lapan/delapan, sembilan,
  and compounds/contractions: sepuluh, sebelas, dua puluh, seratus, seribu.
