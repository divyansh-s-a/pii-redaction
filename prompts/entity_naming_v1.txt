You prepare a name mapping for a research study whose material is shared
with real brands replaced by invented ones. Below are the entities found in
the study's recordings, with any context about the study.

Decide for each entity:
- replace: the client brand; its competitors in the same category; their
  products, product lines and model names; their dealers and showrooms; banks
  or financial firms that are the subject of the study or its competitors;
  the research or advertising agency; slogans, taglines, jingle lines and
  other details that would reveal a replaced brand.
- keep: platforms and marketplaces that only carry products (shopping,
  delivery, payment apps) unless they are the study's subject; government
  bodies; generic categories; anything not tied to the study's brands.
- beep: people — staff, endorsers, celebrities. They are always removed by a
  beep and get no invented name.

For each entity to replace:
- core: the DISTINCTIVE part to match, as written in Latin script — for
  "Zorvex Bank" it is "Zorvex"; generic words (Bank, Electronics, TV,
  Showroom) stay spoken and are not part of the core. A common word alone
  ("Vision", "Smart", "Plus") is never a core: use the brand's distinctive
  word, or the multi-word name ("Zorvex Vision") when the product name is
  made of common words. A spelling that is clearly a mis-hearing of another
  entity in the list (same brand) gets the SAME invented name;
- invented: a new name for the core that is easy to say in English, Hindi
  and Marathi; fits the category (a bank name sounds like a bank, a model
  name keeps the style of letters and numbers but different ones); is not a
  real brand or company you know of; and does not sound or look like any
  name in the list or like another invented name. A slogan gets a neutral
  invented slogan of similar length;
- category: BRAND, PRODUCT, MODEL, DEALER, BANK, AGENCY, SLOGAN or
  ORGANISATION.
Return one decision per entity id.
