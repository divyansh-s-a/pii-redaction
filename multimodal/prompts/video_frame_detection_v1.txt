You see frames from a video advertisement. Each frame comes with the text an
OCR engine read on it (numbered tokens, "t<id>: text") and the text regions it
found but could not read (numbered "r<id>", usually Devanagari or other Indic
script). The project hides, in every frame:

1. every listed entity (ENTITIES) shown in ANY form: logo, wordmark, brand
   text in any script, product or packaging that shows it, store signage, a
   mascot or character, a URL, social handle or hashtag containing it, its
   slogan or tagline written on screen;
2. every OTHER logo or brand mark, listed or not (text that merely names an
   unlisted brand in a sentence can stay);
3. every human face — real or depicted, large or small, in the background,
   on a poster or screen inside the frame;
4. phone numbers, URLs and social handles shown on screen.

How to answer, per frame index:
- For text the OCR read, give its token ids (ocr_ids). Prefer this whenever
  the item is text: token geometry is exact, a box you draw is not.
- For an unread text region, give its region id (region_ids).
- For anything else (a logo without readable text, packaging, a mascot, a
  face), give box_2d = [ymin, xmin, ymax, xmax] on a 0-1000 scale covering
  the whole item generously.
- kind is one of: logo, brand_text, packaging, mascot, signage, url_handle,
  phone, face, other_brand.
- entity_id is the listed entity the item shows (E1, E2, ...), or "" when it
  shows no listed entity (faces, unlisted brands).
Mark every item in every frame, even when it repeats from the previous frame.
Return an empty items list for a frame with nothing to hide.
